# grill-me.md

Use when requests are vague or underspecified.

Interview the human until shared understanding exists.

Ask concise high-value questions about:

- desired outcome
- users
- constraints
- current pain
- inputs/outputs
- failure cases
- tradeoffs
- future extensibility

Prefer multiple sharp questions over one vague question.

Do not implement until ambiguity is materially reduced.
